Privacy

No cookies. No tracking. No analytics. This site does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking scripts, and does not load anything from third-party servers.

What is stored

When you calibrate your screen, the result (how many pixels make a metre on your display, plus the screen size and resolution if you entered them) is saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to calibrate again. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

If local storage is blocked, the simulations still work; you will just need to calibrate again on each visit.

Clearing it

Use "Forget saved scale" in the calibration dialog (under "No card? Use screen size"), or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
